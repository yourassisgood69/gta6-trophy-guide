    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10

    One playthrough, no online trophies, none glitched, no difficulty settings, no mission replay. Free-roam continues after the story. Cheats disable trophies and saving — never use them on a trophy run. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots + 1 autosave).

      ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

      Meditate at all 18 spots across 10 locations — and do the first meditation in Chapter 1, because it may be the game's only missable trophy. After the Chapter 1 mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning (she says she is going to meditate), follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. Meditating as both Jason and Lucia counts twice per spot; the last meditation needs both characters together (after Chapter 2, Coffee Break). Track it at Progress → Collections → Exploration → Meditations. Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide and GTABase.
